# EliteTCG — AI UI Prompt

---

Build a professional, clean Shopify-style e-commerce website for **EliteTCG** — a South African online store selling Pokemon Trading Card Game products. Currency is South African Rand (R).

Products sold: booster boxes, elite trainer boxes, booster packs, singles, accessories, tins & bundles. Products are organized by category and by Pokemon set (e.g. Prismatic Evolutions, Surging Sparks).

### Pages:

1. **Homepage** — shows featured products, shop by category, shop by set, and a section encouraging users to buy & sell on the marketplace

2. **Product listing page** — browse all products with filters

3. **Product detail page** — view a single product, see images, description, reviews, and add to cart

4. **Cart & Checkout** — review cart, enter shipping info, pay

5. **My Orders** — view past orders, click into any order for full details

6. **Order Detail** — see order status, tracking, items ordered, shipping address, and order updates

7. **Login / Register** — sign in or create an account

8. **Track Order** — enter order number and email to check status

9. **Wishlist** — saved products

10. **Search** — search for products

11. **Legal pages** — Terms of Service, Privacy Policy, Refund Policy
